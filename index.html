NODE — 02 · Statement
Comfort is obedience.Trust the tremor.

Pond constructs site-responsive, projector-based audio-visual environments that combine 3D animation, bold typographic commands, music, and reactive sound to interrogate what Pond calls the Reflection Subject.

This is the avoidant, performative, and comfort-seeking identity manufactured by contemporary mechanisms of social control. Working through repeating surreal visual loops, large-scale projection, and spatial glitch, Pond’s installations deconstruct the conceptual mirror installed by institutional and ideological power, exposing an apparatus that distorts human perception so that identity degenerates into compliant performance. By examining how media saturation, normative social scripts, and mutual surveillance compel individuals to surrender raw self-confidence in exchange for systemic certainty, the work dismantles the illusion of a stable self-image. Rather than offering an idealized reflection for passive consumption, these spatial interventions disrupt the comfort of obedience, isolating the tremor of a volatile, unscripted core of organic self-worth and forcing a direct encounter with the ideological structures governing the contemporary gaze.
