NODE — 02 · Statement
Comfort is obedience.Trust the tremor.

Pond builds site-responsive audio-visual environments for projection: 3D animation, bold typographic commands, music, and sound that reacts to the room.

A pond’s surface returns a smooth, borrowed image. Beneath it, life never stops moving. Pond’s work turns away from the reflection we are taught to perform, and toward what lives underneath: the tremor that comfort calls unstable.

NODE — 04 · About
Pond

Pond interrogates the avoidant, performative and comfort-seeking identity manufactured by contemporary mechanisms of social control.

Born in Daegu, Pond spent a school term in Khabarovsk in 2004, then grew up in Los Angeles and Long Beach, where the identity took shape from 2008 to 2020. Before moving into image and sound for rooms, Pond was a musician, writing songs and playing guitar. Now working from Seoul, in English and Korean, both native.
